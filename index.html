How to play

  1. Miners (⛏️) go on the orange/gray ore patches and dig ore out of the ground. Ore leaves through the green output port.
  2. Belts (➡️) carry items. Press R to rotate the ghost before placing, or click-drag to lay a whole line of belts that face the way you drag. Scroll zooms the map in and out. Right-click rotates anything already placed.
  3. Furnaces (🔥) smelt ore into plates. Assemblers (⚙️) craft wire, gears, and circuits — click one with the assembler tool to cycle recipes, or pick the Selector (👆) and click it to choose a recipe directly. Ingredients enter from any side except the green port; finished goods leave through the green port.
  4. Feed finished goods from any side into the delivery hub (📦) — it's a 2×1 shipping crate with a white drop-in badge — to fill the shipment orders on the right. Fill all 6 to win.

Tip: a miner facing a belt facing a furnace facing a belt facing a hub is your first production line. Chain from there.
